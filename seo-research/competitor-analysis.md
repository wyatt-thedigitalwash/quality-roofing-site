# Competitor Analysis: Quality Roofing, Inc.

Research date: July 18, 2026. Method: live web searches run the way a Florida customer searches (21 queries across 7 market groups: Tampa commercial, Tampa general, Orlando, Southwest FL, South FL, North FL, statewide condo/multifamily), followed by direct fetches of each competitor's website (homepage, service pages, location pages, blog, raw page source for JSON-LD). 59 unique companies surfaced; the 12 most strategically relevant were deep-dived. Everything below was directly observed unless labeled as inference.

A note on "rankings": we have no rank-tracking tool. Where we say a company "ranks" or "surfaced," it means they appeared in the organic results of our July 2026 searches. That is real evidence of visibility but not a tracked position over time.

---

## Tier 1: Statewide commercial incumbents

### 1. Advanced Roofing Inc. (advancedroofing.com)

The single most important competitor. Surfaced in ALL six of our regional market groups, including top spots for South Florida commercial and statewide condo/apartment queries.

- Who: Fort Lauderdale HQ, founded 1983, 100% employee-owned, 750+ employees, 7 Florida branches (Fort Lauderdale, Miami, Palm Beach, Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, Fort Myers), bonded to $50M per project. Commercial-only, with a large solar arm. Clients include Fort Lauderdale Airport, University of Miami, Simon Malls.
- Site structure: 15+ service pages (re-roofing, repair, maintenance, coatings, plus differentiators like crane division, drone surveys, lightning protection). Only 8 location pages, one per branch, though the Tampa page is deep (~2,000 words, 16 embedded Google reviews).
- Targeting: keyword-first titles ("Commercial Roofing Contractor Tampa - Repairs and Re-Roofing"). Content targets Florida regulatory queries (15-year roof rule, 25% replacement threshold) and hurricane/claims topics.
- Strong: authority moat (40+ years, awards, institutional clients), 4.6-star Google badge with sourced testimonials, deep location pages.
- Beatable: their #1 visibility for statewide condo/apartment/multifamily queries rests on GENERIC commercial pages. They have no dedicated multifamily, condo association, or apartment service page at all. Blog dormant since April 2025 (144 posts, then silence). Schema is Yoast defaults only (no LocalBusiness, Service, or FAQPage), and their Organization schema contains a broken LinkedIn URL. No city pages beyond the 7 branches: Sarasota, Naples, St. Pete, Lakeland, Tallahassee, Pensacola are all uncovered.

### 2. CentiMark Corporation (centimark.com)

- Who: North America's largest commercial-only roofer, 3,500+ associates, 71 US offices, 5 in Florida. Their "Tampa" office is actually in Lakeland.
- Site structure: 10 service pages plus a full roof-systems hub. 5 Florida location pages only, no Miami page. The best technical SEO in the field: verified LocalBusiness + GeoCoordinates + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage (9 Q&A pairs) JSON-LD on the Tampa location page.
- Blog: genuinely active, 1 to 3 posts per month (June 2026 latest), facility-manager topics (Section 179 tax angles, critical environments).
- Strong: national domain authority, correct location-page schema, active content engine, enterprise trust signals (Single Source Warranty).
- Beatable: zero social proof on site (no testimonials, no review counts, and their branded review SERP is dominated by employee reviews and a 1-star Yelp brand page over a warranty dispute). No aggregateRating schema. Surrounding cities are text mentions, not pages. Tampa proximity signal is weak (Lakeland address). National template, no Florida-specific service content, faceless positioning.

### 3. Tecta America Southeast (tectaamerica.com)

- Who: Florida unit of the national roll-up (~5,500 employees, 110+ locations). 8 Florida offices under FIVE different brands (Tecta Southeast, Tecta West Florida, Tecta South Florida, Empire Roofing, Childers Roofing).
- Site structure: 11 national service pages, 8 Florida office pages (substantial ones: team bios, testimonials from Disney Springs/PCL). Titles are exact-match ("Commercial Roofing in Tampa FL | Tecta America West Florida").
- Blog: active but it is a corporate PR feed (acquisitions, executive promotions). Zero customer-facing or Florida content.
- Strong: brand mass, substantial office pages, institutional credibility.
- Beatable: no city network beyond offices, no LocalBusiness/Service/Review schema (generic Organization only), near-zero public reviews for Florida units (0 HomeAdvisor reviews, non-accredited BBB), and the five-brand fragmentation splits their name recognition and review equity. Orlando is served by a "Sanford" page.

### 4. Baker Roofing Company (bakerroofing.com)

- Who: Raleigh NC giant (est. 1915, formerly #3 nationally), 28 branches, 5 in Florida (Tampa, Orlando, Fort Myers, Jacksonville, Pensacola).
- Site structure: strong commercial service architecture (6 service + 8 industry-vertical + 9 roofing-system pages). Florida location pages are thin (~250 words) with brand-only titles ("Baker Roofing Company of Tampa", no "commercial roofing" or "FL" keyword). Their own breadcrumb schema points to a /florida/ hub that returns 404.
- Blog: very active (10+ posts July 8 to 10, 2026 alone, a bulk push), but zero Florida-localized content.
- Strong: 110-year authority, scalable location template, active blog.
- Beatable: Florida is peripheral (homepage features NC/TN projects only). Thin location pages with unoptimized titles leave "Commercial Roofing Tampa FL" style queries uncontested. No LocalBusiness schema, no on-site reviews anywhere, Yelp brand rating 2.8 from 8 reviews. No suburb coverage.

### 5. TeamCraft Roofing (tcrfg.com)

- Who: Salisbury NC family-owned commercial/industrial roofer, 26 metros across 13 states, 3 in Florida. "Tampa" office is actually in Brooksville (50+ miles out).
- Site structure: 13 service pages + 6 roofing-system education pages, well cross-linked. One page per Florida metro; the ~15 surrounding cities per metro are text listings, not pages.
- Strong: deep service architecture, productized offerings (ShelterCare maintenance program, AI platform), national accounts.
- Beatable: near-zero visible reviews (no Tampa or Orlando review profile surfaced at all), long boilerplate title suffixes, no Service/Breadcrumb schema, and their own employee reviews complain they "open too many offices without proper structure." An out-of-state brand with a Brooksville address cannot out-local a Tampa headquarters.

## Tier 2: Family-legacy rivals (the positioning fight)

### 6. Sutter Roofing Company of Florida (sutterroofing.com)

The closest positioning rival: family-owned since 1902, "Florida's Top Commercial Roofing Contractor," surfaced top-organic in our Tampa commercial searches.

- Who: Sarasota HQ, 300+ employees, offices in Tampa, Orlando, Fort Myers, Jacksonville. Commercial only.
- Site structure: 7 service pages, 5 Florida office pages (~400 to 500 words each). Meta titles are 2012-era keyword stuffing with cities MISMATCHED to pages: the Tampa page is titled "Tampa's Largest Commercial Roofing Business | Roof Maintenance Orlando" and the About page is titled "About Commercial Roofers Tampa | Roofing Repair & Restoration Orlando."
- Strong: the strongest family-heritage claim possible (120+ years), real statewide offices, institutional testimonials (SYSCO, Sarasota Memorial Hospital).
- Beatable: blog dead since October 2022 (Hurricane Ian posts were the last ones). No LocalBusiness or Service schema. No non-office city coverage. No Google review counts or badges anywhere on site, and their branded SERP is polluted by an unrelated California "Sutter Roofing Systems." They are coasting on domain age. In Tampa specifically, "Tampa's own third-generation firm" beats "Sarasota company with a Tampa branch."

### 7. McEnany Roofing (mcenanyroofing.com)

The Tampa home-market incumbent to displace.

- Who: Tampa HQ (8801 Industrial Dr), family-owned since 1985, offices in Lake Alfred and Sarasota. Commercial and tile only.
- Site structure: 10 service/vertical pages (condo, hospital, retail-office). ZERO location or service-area pages. Meta descriptions literally duplicate the title tag (verified: the condo page description is "Condo Roofing | McEnany Roofing"). Homepage has 0 JSON-LD blocks; the only schema found anywhere was a single VideoObject.
- Blog: dormant since 2023; main archive is a 2017-2018 burst.
- Strong: 40 years of domain age, 9 named property-manager testimonials, marquee projects (Kennedy Space Center, Florida Aquarium), three real offices.
- Beatable: hollow SEO foundation (no geo pages, no schema, dead blog, duplicated metas) plus a weak third-party review profile (Birdeye 2.9 stars across 73 reviews). They rank on age, not effort. This is the most directly displaceable Tampa commercial competitor.

### 8. Crowther Roofing & Cooling (crowther.net)

- Who: Fort Myers institution, five generations since 1955 (in FL since 1975), 900+ employees, ~$181M revenue, offices in Fort Myers, Sarasota, Jupiter. Commercial-heavy plus HVAC.
- Site structure: 12 service pages, only 4 to 5 office pages, /locations index returns 404. Titles are brand-led with no city keywords ("Expert Commercial Roof Replacements & Repairs").
- Schema: unusually thorough homepage JSON-LD (HomeAndConstructionBusiness with AggregateRating 4.5/464 reviews, credentials, OfferCatalogs), but reviews are invisible on the rendered pages.
- Blog: 2 to 4 posts per year, homeowner-focused despite commercial dominance.
- Beatable: they only rank where they have buildings. No Tampa presence or mention at all, zero city-specific pages, un-optimized titles. Not a threat in Tampa Bay; a benchmark to out-structure in SWFL.

## Tier 3: The niche and structural specialists (the playbooks to study)

### 9. Colonial Roofing, Inc. (colonialroofing.com)

The programmatic local SEO benchmark in the field, and an active invader of Tampa Bay.

- Who: Lehigh Acres HQ, ~50 years claimed, offices in Sarasota and Largo (their Tampa Bay beachhead). Condo association and commercial focus, PGA golfer (Chris DiMarco) TV endorsement.
- Site structure: 104 city landing pages counted on their locations index, covering Gulf Coast plus DENSE Tampa Bay coverage (Wesley Chapel, Carrollwood, Westchase, Brandon, Apollo Beach, Lutz, Land O Lakes, and dozens more). Verified city-page schema stack: RoofingContractor, City, Service, FAQPage. Segment pages target condo associations, community association managers (CAMs), and commercial property managers, which matches how multifamily buying decisions actually happen.
- Weak: city pages live at /blog/roofing-contractor-[city] URLs (blog-path, likely templated, with live slug 404s), NO review proof anywhere (their visible third-party listing is 21 reviews at 3 stars on Birdeye), anonymous About page (no leadership, no founding year), blog stalled since April 15, 2026.
- Lesson: their architecture (city grid + audience-segment pages + full schema) is the play worth copying. Their thin trust layer is the vulnerability: a firm with a real Tampa HQ, real reviews, and a real family story executing the same architecture beats them in Hillsborough/Pinellas.

### 10. Biscayne Roofing and Waterproofing (biscayneroofing.com)

Currently holding the statewide multifamily rankings almost by default.

- Who: Davie FL (Broward) family firm, "Serving Florida Since 1965" claimed (BBB says 29 years). Commercial-first plus waterproofing.
- Site structure: ~15 service pages including THREE dedicated multifamily segment pages (condo/apartment, HOA, multi-family), which is why they surfaced in our statewide condo searches. ~51 city pages, all South Florida tri-county. Zero pages, offices, or mentions for Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville, or the Gulf Coast.
- Weak: the condo page that ranks statewide is ~850 words with UNFILLED FAQ template placeholders, and contains no mention of SB-4D, milestone inspections, or SIRS (the exact regulatory pain driving Florida condo re-roofs since Surfside). Zero JSON-LD on the homepage. Their /reviews page displays zero actual reviews. Blog has one visible post.
- Lesson: the statewide multifamily SERP is held by a thin tri-county company. This is the single most winnable high-value territory we found.

### 11. Best Roofing / Gustafson Industries (bestroofing.net)

- Who: Fort Lauderdale, founded 1978, commercial/industrial/multifamily. Runs "Best Roofing University," an approved CEU provider for licensed Florida CAMs, a genuinely clever moat with condo decision-makers.
- Site structure: 10 service pages, 6 region-level pages (Tampa, Orlando, Jacksonville among them). The Tampa page is substantively localized (FL Building Code wind loads, 15 named sub-areas) but carries no Tampa address, and their own JSON-LD declares only Fort Lauderdale and Miami locations. Messy URLs (four-level nesting, /our-service vs /our-services inconsistencies, schema leaking the internal label "2026 Homepage - New").
- Weak: no suburb pages anywhere, low review volume for a 48-year firm (29 Yelp, 27 Facebook), and despite claiming multifamily as a core segment they have NO dedicated condo/HOA service page.
- Lesson: renting Tampa relevance from 250 miles away. Also proof that CAM/condo-board relationship content works; Quality Roofing can build the Tampa-native version.

### 12. SPC Jacksonville Commercial Roofers (jacksonvillecommercialroofers.com)

- Who: SPC Roofers LLC brand site, Jacksonville, since 1978. Exact-match domain. Claims Duro-Last Platinum + Owens Corning Platinum exclusivity in NE Florida.
- Site structure: only 13 pages total, but service pages are DEEP: their TPO page is ~4,200 words with FAQs and a complete JSON-LD stack (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, Organization, BreadcrumbList). Zero location pages; several nav "services" are actually dated blog posts.
- Strong: ~370+ reviews at 4.8 claimed for the SPC brand, BBB A+ accredited, long-form service pages.
- Lesson: their 4,200-word system pages are the service-page depth benchmark. Their zero-geo-page architecture means everything outside downtown Jacksonville is open.

## Landscape summary: what this market rewards and where the openings are

What the winners actually have (observed patterns across the 12):

1. Location pages win visibility, but almost everyone stops at their office cities. Advanced (8), Baker (5), CentiMark (5), Sutter (5), Tecta (8), Crowther (4). Only Colonial (104) and Biscayne (51) run real city grids, and each is confined to one coast. NOBODY runs a statewide city grid. The suburb long tail (St. Pete, Clearwater, Brandon, Lakeland, Sarasota, Naples, Kissimmee, and dozens more) is nearly uncontested for commercial queries.
2. The multifamily/condo segment is the largest undefended prize. Advanced holds statewide condo visibility with generic pages; Biscayne holds it with an 850-word template that never mentions SB-4D. Post-Surfside regulation (milestone inspections, SIRS reserve studies) created a compliance-driven search behavior that no competitor's content serves. Best Roofing's CAM University shows the audience is reachable and valuable.
3. Schema is a differentiator because most skip it. Only CentiMark (location pages), Colonial (city pages), SPC (service pages), and Crowther (homepage) have real stacks. Zero competitors combine LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage + aggregateRating consistently.
4. Blogs are dormant or non-local. Sutter dead since 2022, McEnany since 2023, Advanced since April 2025, Tecta is PR-only, Baker and Crowther publish non-Florida content. Only CentiMark and SPC publish relevant commercial content with any consistency. A Florida-specific commercial blog (hurricane, insurance claims, SB-4D, 15-year rule, Section 179) has almost no active competition.
5. Review proof is weak everywhere. On-site review integration exists only at Advanced. CentiMark, Baker, Tecta, TeamCraft, Colonial, Biscayne all show zero on-site review counts. The bar for "best-reviewed statewide commercial roofer" is low.
6. Family legacy is contested but winnable. Sutter (1902) and Crowther (1955) have longer stories, but neither tells it well online (Sutter's About has no names or faces; Colonial's is anonymous). A third-generation story with real faces, names, and project dollars attached would be the best-TOLD legacy story in the field, if not the oldest.

The openings, ranked:

1. Statewide multifamily/condo content and pages (vs Biscayne's thin template and Advanced's generic pages), anchored in SB-4D/milestone/SIRS compliance content.
2. Tampa Bay commercial dominance (vs McEnany's hollow SEO, Sutter's branch office, Baker/CentiMark/TeamCraft's remote addresses): Quality Roofing is the only $29M-scale commercial roofer with an actual Tampa headquarters and a modern site.
3. The statewide city-page grid nobody has built, phased out from Tampa.
4. Schema plus review-engine as table stakes that most of the field skips.
